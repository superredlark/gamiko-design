## 🌟 About the Project (English)

**An emerging cross-platform hub for casual games and interactive visual novels.**

Gamiko is a cross-platform application dedicated to providing players with a diverse collection of premium, offline-first casual games and interactive visual novels. 

Beyond being a gaming hub, Gamiko is an open-collaboration ecosystem. We warmly invite players, storytellers, artists, and developers to join us in discussing and shaping the future of this platform. Whether you are interested in narrative design, visual arts, or core coding, let's build a vibrant entertainment and storytelling experience together.

---

## 🚀 Current Status & Try It Out

Gamiko is a passion project currently in its early stages. We are actively iterating and improving the experience!

* **Android Users:** You can find and play the latest version by searching for "Gamiko" on the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=studio.src.gamiko).
* **iOS Users:** Our app is currently under review by Apple. We hope to be live on the App Store very soon—stay tuned!

## 🤝 How to Collaborate

Gamiko is a growing ecosystem. We welcome creative minds to help us build this platform.

* **Storytellers:** Bring your mythological or creative scripts to life through our interactive narrative engine.
* **Visual Artists:** Showcase your character designs or background art within our game modules.
* **Game Visionaries:** Have a unique game idea? Whether it's a rough sketch or a detailed design, we are open to exploring new concepts.
* **Experience Analysts:** Help us refine Gamiko by providing UX insights, reporting bugs, or suggesting balance adjustments via GitHub Issues.
* **Sound Designers (Upcoming):** We plan to add audio support soon and will collaborate on BGM and sound effects.
* **Game Module Developers (Upcoming):** Once our internal development tools are ready, we will invite developers to create independent game modules within the Gamiko platform.
* **Promoters & Marketers (Upcoming):** We are designing a referral system to track promoter IDs. A portion of the revenue generated by referred users will be shared with the promoters.

## 💰 Revenue Sharing & Incentives

As Gamiko has just launched, we do not have a formal revenue-sharing formula yet. Current contributions are based on shared interest and passion. However, we are committed to rewarding our contributors:

1.  **Contribution Tracking:** Every contribution (via PRs or other documented channels) will be recorded to build a "Contribution Index" for each participant.
2.  **Future Dividends:** Once the platform starts generating steady revenue (e.g., reaching the first $100 milestone), we will formalize a transparent calculation strategy to distribute profits based on the Contribution Index.
3.  **Join the Discussion:** If you have expertise or ideas regarding fair incentive models for independent creators, we warmly welcome your input in our Discussion area.

---

## 🛠 How to Participate

We provide a transparent participation process to ensure every contribution is accurately recorded as a basis for future incentives:

1.  **Direct Contribution (GitHub Workflow):**
    * **Step 1:** Send a quick email to tell us your **preferred private branch name**.
    * **Step 2:** I will create a dedicated private branch for you within this repository to avoid conflicts and manage your contributions independently.
    * **Step 3:** You can then Fork this repository, complete your work in your local environment, and submit a Pull Request (PR) to your assigned branch.
    * This workflow creates a permanent, timestamped record, which serves as the core evidence for calculating your "Contribution Index."
    * Note: Each asset directory contains a standalone README.md explaining the specific purpose, calling logic, and storage requirements for the files within.

2.  **GitHub Discussions:** Perfect for brainstorming, sharing game ideas, or discussing the revenue-sharing model.

3.  **Email Inquiry:** For private proposals or complex collaboration interests, please contact us via the email address listed below.

## 💰 收益分成与激励

由于 Gamiko 刚刚发布，我们尚未制定正式的收益分成公式。目前的贡献基于共同的兴趣和热情。但是，我们致力于奖励我们的贡献者：

1.  **贡献追踪:** 每一份贡献（通过 PR 或其他记录渠道）都将被记录，为每位参与者建立“贡献指数”。
2.  **未来分红:** 一旦平台开始产生稳定收益（例如达到第一个 100 美元里程碑），我们将制定透明的计算策略，根据贡献指数分配利润。
3.  **加入讨论:** 如果你在独立创作者公平激励模型方面有专业见解或想法，我们非常欢迎你在讨论区提出。

---

## 🛠 如何参与

我们提供透明的参与流程，确保每一份贡献都被准确记录，作为未来激励的依据：

1.  **直接贡献 (GitHub 工作流):**
    * **步骤 1:** 发送一封简短的邮件，告诉我们你**心仪的私有分支名称**。
    * **步骤 2:** 我将在该仓库中为你创建一个专有的私有分支，以避免冲突并独立管理你的贡献。
    * **步骤 3:** 然后你可以 Fork 本仓库，在本地完成工作，并向你分配的分支提交 Pull Request (PR)。
    * 该工作流会创建带有时间戳的永久记录，作为计算“贡献指数”的核心依据。
    * 注意：每个资产目录都包含一个独立的 README.md，解释了该目录下文件的具体用途、调用逻辑和存储要求。

2.  **GitHub Discussions:** 适合头脑风暴、分享游戏点子或讨论收益分成模型。

3.  **邮件咨询:** 对于私密提案或复杂的合作意向，请通过下方列出的邮件地址联系我们。
